# 📚 Gender on the Bestseller List — 90 Years of Stories

A data-driven scrollytelling project visualizing the evolution of gender representation on the New York Times Bestseller List from 1931 to 2024. 

This interactive narrative explores the deep inequalities of early literary establishments, the shifting paradigms of the 70s and 80s, and the genre-defining "Queens" who dominated the charts for decades.

## Features

- **Interactive Scrollytelling:** Fluid vertical scrolling experience that guides the user through historical chapters.
- **Dynamic D3.js Visualizations:** Custom, animated charts (bar, donut, area, and lollipop charts) that build themselves as they enter the viewport.
- **Rich Tooltips:** Deep dive into specific author stats and book metadata by hovering over visualizations.
- **Responsive Design:** Ensures a seamless storytelling experience across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Filterable Data:** Explore the dominance of specific genres and their relation to prestigious literary prizes.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & CSS3:** Semantic structure with custom CSS properties for theme handling (warm, light, dark, and light-purple contrast modes).
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** Native Intersection Observers for scroll-driven animations and event handling.
- **D3.js (v7):** For binding data and creating all SVG-based data visualizations.

## Data Source

Data compiled and analyzed from the historical **New York Times Bestseller List Archive (1931–2024)**, cross-referenced with Pulitzer Prize records. Over 60,000 entries across 7,431 unique titles were processed for this visualization.
